      FAQs

      Frequently Asked Questions

      How do I prepare for a home blood sample collection?

      Most routine tests require 8–12 hours of fasting with plain water allowed. Our team confirms your specific fasting window when the booking is confirmed. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before the test and continue your regular medications unless your doctor advises otherwise. Wear a loose, short-sleeved shirt for easy access.

      How will I receive my test reports?

      Reports are delivered digitally on WhatsApp and SMS within 24 hours for most tests — often same-day for routine panels like CBC, thyroid and lipid profile. You can also download PDF copies anytime from this website using the "Download Report" option.

      What are the charges for home collection?

      Home sample collection is FREE for all health packages and for bookings above a minimal spend (currently ₹399). A small nominal fee of ₹50 applies only for individual tests below that threshold. There are no hidden charges ever.

      Are your lab test reports valid for all doctors?

      Yes. All tests are processed at NABL-accredited partner laboratories with stringent quality control. Our reports are accepted by doctors, hospitals and insurance providers across India.

      How do I schedule a home collection appointment?

      Use the booking form on this page, click the WhatsApp button, or call our helpline at +91 98765 43210. We confirm your slot in under 15 minutes and can usually collect the same day.
